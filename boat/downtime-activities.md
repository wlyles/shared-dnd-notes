# Downtime Activities on the Ship

Here are some ideas for things you can do with downtime while on a ship, since there's potentially a lot of traveling by sea in this campaign. Downtime rules originate from DMG 14, and downtime options presented here come from either PHB 24 (crafting non-magical equipment) or XGE (everything else). This doc does not fully specify the rules, and is only intended to give you some high-level information and offer modifications to suggested downtime activities if you undertake them on the ship.

## General Rules for Downtime Activities (DMG 14)

>Between adventures, the DM might ask you what your character is doing during his or her downtime. Periods of downtime can vary in duration, but each downtime activity requires a certain number of days to complete before you gain any benefit, and at least 8 hours of each day must be spent on the downtime activity for the day to count. The days do not need to be consecutive. If you have more than the minimum amount of days to spend, you can keep doing the same thing for a longer period of time, or switch to a new downtime activity.

>Downtime activities other than the ones presented below are possible. If you want your character to spend his or her downtime performing an activity not covered here, discuss it with your DM.

## Crafting Equipment (PHB 24)

Crafting equipment works exactly as [described in the PHB](https://5e.tools/book.html#xphb,6,crafting%20equipment), with the following notes:

- Most basic materials will be available through Guex, and you'll be able to make anything covered in PHB 24 with what he has in stock. You'll still have to pay of course - Guex is not going to bankroll you and has to get the supplies somehow! He'll keep things in stock in the background for you too.
- If you want to brew higher level potions of healing, we'll be using the table from XGE's "Crafting an Item" downtime activity below (but otherwise PHB 24 will supersede anything in XGE for crafting):

## Other Downtime Activities (XGE)

Here are some other ideas for [downtime activities taken from XGE](https://5e.tools/book.html#xge,2,downtime%20revisited). You can refer to those rules for more information on specifics, I'll just briefly summarize them here and point out how the activity differs if you attempt it on a ship. Of note is that XGE introduces the concept of complications to downtime activities, which have a 10% chance of occurring regardless of the outcome. Most complications from XGE don't make sense in the confines of the ship, so the actual outcomes won't match what you see in the book and are briefly mentioned here.

### Crafting Magic Items

Crafting magic items is a complicated task and requires a lot of time and effort. You will need:
- A formula that describes how to craft the item
- Time and money as described in the table below. The monetary cost covers materials, tools, and so on that Guex provides, similar to the crafting rules above. Both costs are halved for consumable items.
- Proficiency with the relevant tool or the Arcana skill
- One or more exotic materials which Guex can't provide that will require an adventure to acquire. See the table below for the recommended level at which you'd be able to successfully undertake such an adventure

#### Magic Item Crafting Time and Cost
Item Rarity|Workweeks|Cost|Recommended Level
-|-|-|-
Common|1|50 gp|1+
Uncommon|2|200 gp|4+
Rare|10|2,000 gp|9+
Very rare|25|20,000 gp|13+
Legendary|50|100,000 gp|19+

Complications from crafting magical items are inherent to the undertaking required to acquire exotic materials.

### Gambling

Gambling requires one workweek of effort and a stake of at least 10gp. The maximum stake and winnings will depend on how loaded the crew is, and you'll know before you commit.

Gambling requires Insight, Deception, and Intimidation checks. You can replace **one** of these (not all of them - a deviation from XGE) with an appropriate gaming tool proficiency check. The number of success will determine how much debt or winnings you accrue.

Complications from gambling on the ship usually have to do with cheating accusations and angry opponents who want their money back.

### Pit Fighting

Pit Fighting requires one workweek of effort and no monetary investment. The winnings will depend on how loaded the crew is, and you'll know before you commit.

Pit Fighting requires Athletics, Acrobatics, and a Constitution check. You can replace **one** of these with a weapon attack roll. The number of success will determine your winnings, and total failure only costs you time. Note that pit fighting is non-lethal.

Complications from pit fighting on the ship usually have to do with cheating or fixing accusations, angry opponents who lost against you, angry spectators who lost bets because of you, and serious injuries to you or your crewmates.

### Relaxation

Relaxation only takes time - one workweek of "effort".

There are no checks required for relaxation, and you'll have an easier time shaking off any long-lasting negative effects you might have accrued.

The are generally no complications as a result of relaxing, but complications from your past might still rear their ugly heads...

### Religious Service

Religious service requires one workweek of effort. It normally also requires access to a temple or similar, but religious service on the ship will just require a Chaplain. Note that your original Chaplain was laid off, so you need to find a suitable replacement!

Religious service requires a Religion or Persuasion check. Because you don't have full access to religious facilities on a ship, the rewards for religious service will not be as great, but you can still earn up to two "favors" which you can redeem later. These will generally be minor interventions from your deity rather than assistance from temple representatives.

Because of the reduced scope and rewards of religious service on the ship, complications won't arise as a direct result of religious service.

### Research

Research requires one workweek of effort and at least 50gp spent on materials, bribes, gifts, etc. Research typically requires access to a library or sage, but on the ship Guex can help you. He has a lot of knowledge from his tomes and *other* sources that can be beneficial. He's greedy so he's still going to charge you. There might even be lore reasons for how he has access to this knowledge and why he still charges you that you could uncover...

Research requires an Intelligence check with a bonus based on how much you spend on it. There are certain topics about which you would have enough information to gain advantage on the check, but I won't reveal what those topics are. The result of the check determines how many pieces of lore or information you get about the subject of your research.

There are complications that can arise from using Guex's help, but your characters would not necessarily know what they are.

### Training

Training requires ten workweeks total, reduced by a number of weeks equal to your Intelligence modifier (if positive). Training costs 25gp per workweek for materials or instruction.

Training does not require any skill checks, but you may attempt to use the language or tool you're learning before you've learned everything about it. Doing so will require an additional skill check, or increase the difficulty of an associated check according to the following table:

Time Left (rounded up)|DC|Description
-|-|-
10 wk|25|You have superficial knowledge of what you're trying to do, and can haphazardly attempt to use the language or tool you're learning. Getting a successful outcome is very hard, and there might be unintended consequences
9 wk|24
8 wk|22
7 wk|20|You're starting to understand the basics of the language or tool you're learning. It's still hard to do, but with a bit of luck or skill you can accomplish something useful
6 wk|18
5 wk|16
4 wk|15|You have a decent grasp of the language or tool you're learning, and with some moderate difficulty you're able to produce decent results
3 wk|14
2 wk|12
1 wk|10|You are almost proficient with the language or tool you're learning, and using it is becoming easy for you

Complications from training arise when you try to use the skill prematurely. For example, if you try to craft something you might have one of the following outcomes:
- You fail to craft the item, and render some or all of the materials you used useless
- You succeed in creating something, but it's not what you expected. It might be a lower quality version of what you were trying to make, or it might be something completely different. You might not be able to tell that you failed either!

## Other Downtime Activities

These are custom downtime activities for our campaign!

### Fishing

Fishing requires one workweek of effort and fishing equipment.

Fishing requires a Survival check. Success means that you fish up a random catch. You may also make an optional Nature check to target something specific, with the result determining your chance of getting what you want. Your catch can be used for food to reduce the cost of supporting your lifestyle.

Complications from fishing arise when fishing up dangerous creatures that either fight back or poison those who eat them.
