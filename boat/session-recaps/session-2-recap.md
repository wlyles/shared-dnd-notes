# Session 2 Recap

We began with a pirate ambush in the Korinn Archipelago! The pirate ship appeared out of the fog and pulled alongside the Golden Gander, laying down planks for enemy pirates to cross. The pirate captain was also involved with his squid-like gun and two pet legsharks. Bliiploolp and Rix laid down spellfire from the poop deck, with Devdan's help as a walrus against the leg sharks. Vish held the line at one of the planks while Ragnar threw Ffloki up into the crow's nest to rain bolts down from above before going down himself. Lorelei was previously sent down to the surgeon's office and was distracted, but soon caught wind of the struggle and came up to assist. The captain didn't put up much of a fight against Rix's Witch Bolt and Chromatic Orb, though he did give Rix quite the fright.

After the battle, the captain reappeared from his quarters and ordered the crew to salvage any goods from the ship before scuttling it. Our party found a few noteworthy items aboard: 300gp, the pirate captain's squid gun (which appears to be sentient and nearly put Vish down), and three pieces of a treasure map (one of which was poo-stained). The party returned to find that some of their crew had been slain in the fray: two seamen and one topman.

Having dealt with the pirates, the voyage continued on its way to Caer Callidyrr, which was another week away. During the downtime of the voyage, Vish and Lorelei began training with an Herbalism Kit from Devdan, Rix began learning how to play dice with Gunnar, and Bliiploolp began undertaking religious rites. Guex also did some preliminary research on the items the party got from the pirate vessel, determining that the map had some kind of encoded information in it and the squid-like gun was part of some warlock pact.

Upon reaching Caer Callidyrr, the party noticed something was awry. Strange mutant fish had washed ashore on the beach, and the docks were empty - unusual for such an important trade destination and the capital of the United Moonshae Isles. Only one dockhand came to greet them, but he struggled to remember anything and soon fell asleep. A shore party was assembled with Vish, Rix, Bliiploolp, Ragnar, and Guex to investigate and see if the Golden Gander would be able to offload her cargo. The shore party found the city to be largely empty - again strange for a capital city and trade hub. One shop remained open - a magic shop, where the party learned that the shopkeeper had set up a ward and noticed people started falling asleep a week ago. With little else to go on, the party continued to the castle, where they found the gate ajar and the guards asleep.

Moving to the inner keep they met Hankerton, the captain of the guard. He informed the party that the keep was warded as well, and was convinced to direct them to the throne room, where the group convinced another set of guards to let them pass. In the throne room the party encountered High Queen Alicia Kendrick, who was strangely afflicted with the same forgetfulness as the dockhand before he fell asleep despite being in the warded keep. The queen's advisor was also present, and after some back and forth where the queen mentioned Myrloch Vale the advisor had enough and enchanted the party to leave the room. Ragnar and Vish were not so easily swayed, but thought it best to leave for now and see what the druids had to say.

Upon returning to the ship though, the party witnessed an ongoing mutiny led by Snagglefang and Gristlegut. The party managed to talk them into binding the captain and burying him "nipple deep" in the beach sand rather than killing him. The party also left a note taped to him that read "dearest hanky, this guy's a piece of shit if you find him please put him in the brig - your dearest friends, xoxo". In the power vacuum, a democratic system was adopted and Ragnar was elected leader. Ragnar consulted with his officers to see where to go next, with the most promising plan being to head north to the nearby port town of White Rock or Cobh to assess the extent of the sleeping curse, then traveling south and west to either Caervu or Dultann before traveling inland to Myrloch Vale. Other proposals included voyages to Manger or even Caer Corwell to reduce time spent on foot.
