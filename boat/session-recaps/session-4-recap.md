# Session 4 Recap

After a breakthrough on the puzzle for the treasure of Cap Tain, the crew pulled a handbrake turn and headed towards Tir Faoi Thoinn to try to find it! They were able to locate the coastline shown on the map and dove in the water where they noticed a shipwreck. When they got closer, they were attacked by a coven of sea hags and their octopus pet. The sea hags proved dangerous by conjuring a lightning bolt that stuck the party, but in the end were no match. The party investigated the shipwreck and found a passage into a cave within. The cave appeared to have been previously explored or perhaps inhabited, but was now filled with ghouls. A chuul guarded the treasure in the final room. The party was able to dispatch all of these foes and found the treasure - Cap Tain's gunsword, tricorn, and necklace!
