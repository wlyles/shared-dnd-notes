# Magic Items

This document contains all non-standard magic item descriptions.

## Cap Tain's Gunsword
*Weapon (Rapier), Artifact (Requires Attunement)*<br>
*Martial Weapon, Melee Weapon*<br>
1d8 Piercing<br>
Finesse<br>
5lbs<br>
Mastery: Double Tap<br>

***One-of-a-Kind Weapon.*** Cap Tain's Gunsword is a one-of-a-kind weapon that's difficult to wield. All other features of this weapon don't function unless your offhand is empty, as using this weapon requires intense focus and balance.

***Finesse.*** When making an attack with a Finesse weapon, use your choice of your Strength or Dexterity modifier for the attack and damage rolls. You must use the same modifier for both rolls.

***Magic Weapon.*** Cap Tain's Gunsword is a magic weapon that grants a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with it.

***Integrated Firearm.*** When attacking with Cap Tain's Gunsword, you may choose to use either the rapier or the pistol integrated into the hilt of the weapon. This integrated pistol functions exactly like a normal pistol, including the pistol's loading and mastery properties. It also has the same *Magic Weapon* feature mentioned above. Additionally, when you attack with either part of this weapon, you can make one extra attack as a Bonus Action later on the same turn with either weapon. You don't add your ability modifier to the extra attack's damage unless that modifier is negative. *(DM note: custom version of Dual Wielder)*

***Mastery: Double Tap.*** When you attack with either the rapier or the integrated pistol, you can make an attack with the other weapon as part of the Attack action. You can make this extra attack only once per turn. *(DM note: custom version of Mastery: Nick)*

## Tricorn of Advanced Piracy
*Wondrous Item, Artifact (Requires Attunement)*

***Superior Intellect.*** While you wear this hat, your Intelligent score increases by half your level (rounded down), up to a maximum of 20.

## Amulet of the Moonshaes
*Wondrous Item, Artifact (Requires Attunement)*<br>
1lb

***Superior Spellcasting.*** While you wear this amulet, you gain a +1 bonus to spell attack rolls and the saving throw DCs of your spells.
